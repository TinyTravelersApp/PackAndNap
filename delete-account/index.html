Delete your Pack & Nap account

App: Pack & Nap · Developer: Pack&Nap LLC

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Pack & Nap and sign in.
  2. Open your account settings and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you may be asked to confirm with Apple so we can also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

We start deleting your account as soon as you confirm, and you are signed out on this device. Your data is removed from our live systems within 30 days.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

You don't need to reinstall the app. Send an email:

To: Pack.Nap.App@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my Pack & Nap account
Send from: the email address on your Pack & Nap account

Please include:

  • The email address on your account (and how you sign in: email and password, Apple, or Google).
  • If you used "Hide My Email" with Sign in with Apple, the relay address (ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com) if you know it.
  • Whether you want the whole account deleted, or only certain data (for example photos, documents, a child's profile, or your emergency card).

To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from the account's email address. If you can't send from that address, we will email that address to confirm the request before deleting anything. We will never ask for your password.

Timeframe

  • We reply to email requests within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days of confirming the request. We email you when it's done.
  • Remaining copies in backups are removed within 90 days of deletion.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first so you aren't charged again:

  • iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
  • Android: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

Delete only some data

You can keep your account and delete individual items in the app: a family member, a trip, a photo, a document, a recap, or your emergency card details. You can also ask us by email.
