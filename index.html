About the App

Pack & Nap helps parents of kids from newborn to teens plan family trips with less stress. Create age-specific packing lists, manage nap and meal schedules, organize trip details, and get practical AI tips — all in one place.

  • Age-specific packing lists for each child
  • Daily nap, meal, and bedtime schedules
  • Trip organization (itinerary, bookings, photo diary)
  • AI travel tips and kid-friendly suggestions
  • Family sharing
  • Optional Premium plan
