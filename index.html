About the App

Pack & Nap helps parents of infants, toddlers, and preschoolers plan family trips with less stress. Create age-specific packing lists, manage nap and meal schedules, organize trip details, and get practical AI tips — all in one place.

  • Age-specific packing lists for each child
  • Daily nap, meal, and bedtime schedules
  • Trip organization (itinerary, bookings, documents, photo diary)
  • AI travel tips and kid-friendly suggestions
  • Family sharing
  • Optional Premium plan

Privacy Policy

Pack & Nap collects only the information needed to provide the service:

  • Account information (email address)
  • Trip details and kids’ profiles (including ages and nap times)
  • Documents and photos you choose to upload

Your data is stored securely and is only shared with people you explicitly invite to a trip. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app.

We do not sell your personal information.
