Pack & Nap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

  • Pack & Nap is an app for parents and guardians (18+) to plan family trips, packing lists and nap schedules for kids from newborn to 17.
  • You decide what to add. Some of it is about your children, and some can be health-related (allergies, medical notes). Those health fields are optional.
  • We use your information to run the app. We do not sell your personal information.
  • AI features send trip and family details to Anthropic (Claude) only after you agree on a consent screen. You can turn AI suggestions off at any time. If you don't agree, you still get ready-made packing lists by age, with no AI involved.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app or at our account deletion page.

1. Who we are

Pack & Nap is provided by Pack&Nap LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("we", "us"). We are responsible for (the "controller" of) the personal information described in this policy.

Contact: Pack.Nap.App@gmail.com

2. Who the app is for

Pack & Nap is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children or teens, and no one under 18 may create an account. Parents and legal guardians can add information about their children, from newborns up to age 17, so the app can build age-appropriate packing lists, routines and trip plans. Kids' profiles are for children under 18. People aged 18 or over are added as adults. In this policy, "child" means anyone under 18.

When you sign in, the app shows: "By continuing, you agree to our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy."

5. Health information

Allergies, medical notes, insurance and doctor details are optional. We collect them only if you enter them, and only to show them to you and to invited trip companions (adults with a verified email), for example so a grandparent has a child's allergy list on hand.

  • The first time you save allergies, medical, insurance or doctor details, the app shows a one-time acknowledgement. You confirm you understand before those details are saved, and we record that acknowledgement with a timestamp.
  • You can edit or delete health details at any time.
  • Health details, including allergies, medical notes, insurance and doctor details, are never sent to AI. Emergency contacts are never sent to AI either.
  • We never sell health details.
  • Washington residents and others covered by consumer health data laws: see our Consumer Health Data notice.

The app gives practical travel tips. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for advice from your child's doctor.

6. Children's information

Pack & Nap is not directed to children (anyone under 18), and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about children from newborn to age 17 (such as names, birth dates, nap times, allergies and photos) is entered by their parent or legal guardian, who controls it. Kids' profiles are for children under 18. People aged 18 or over are added as adults.

  • COPPA. We do not let children create accounts or give us information themselves. If we learn that anyone under 18 has created an account, or that a child under 13 has given us personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you think this has happened, email Pack.Nap.App@gmail.com.
  • Review, edit or delete. In the app you can view and edit each child's profile at any time, delete a family member (which removes their profile), delete photos, or delete your whole account. You can also email us to review or delete your child's information.
  • Teens (13 to 17). Parents and guardians may add teens to the family so they get age-appropriate packing lists and plans. Teens do not get their own accounts, and we treat their information with the same care as younger children's: we use it only for the features the parent uses, do not sell it, and share it only with people the parent invites. Where UK or EU law (including the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and GDPR) or US state laws on minors' data give teens rights of their own, a teen can contact us directly to see or delete information about them.
  • Minimum use. We use children's information only to provide the features you use. AI features receive a child's age group and age, not their name or birth date.
  • Sharing. Invites are for adults only. Adults you invite to a trip (with a verified email) can see children's information on that trip, including the trip's emergency info and each traveler's allergies and medical notes. People you send a recap link to can view that recap and its photos. Children's information is never sold.

7. AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence: nap-friendly day plans, AI packing lists, trip ideas, activity and restaurant ideas, and trip recaps.

  • Provider. These requests are processed by Anthropic (the Claude models), through our app platform provider Emergent.
  • Your consent. Nothing is sent to AI until you agree on a consent screen, which offers "Agree and continue" and "Not now". The screen lists what is sent and what is never sent. You can also turn "AI suggestions" on or off in your account settings at any time. If you don't agree, or you turn AI off, you still get ready-made packing lists by age, with no AI involved. Every time you agree, decline or withdraw consent is logged with a timestamp. Deleting your account deletes that log.
  • What is sent. Destination, dates, weather, each traveler's age group and age (or an adult's role), routine times, and plans, bookings and packing items where needed. Trip recaps also send photo captions, not the photos. The consent screen lists these details.
  • What is not sent. AI does not receive names or birth dates from family profiles, email addresses, trip names, allergies or medical notes, photos, confirmation numbers, notes, emergency contacts, or insurance and doctor details.
  • Text you type. Captions, plan and booking titles, and packing items are sent as you wrote them, so they could still contain a name. The consent screen says this too.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. Please check them, especially for safety, health and timing.

People you choose. People you invite to a trip see that trip, including the family members on it. Invited trip companions (adults with a verified email) can also see the trip's emergency info and each traveler's allergies and medical notes. Invites are for adults only. A recap share link lets the people you send it to view that recap and its photos until the link expires after 30 days or you turn it off.

9. Booking and affiliate links

The Plan tab offers links to travel booking partners (for example for flights, hotels and activities). When you tap one, your browser opens the partner's site with the destination, dates and number of travellers (and children's ages where needed) filled in. The partner's own privacy policy then applies. Some links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account, family, trips and photos: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • After account deletion: removed from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
  • Recap share links: expire after 30 days.
  • Document uploads (removed): The app no longer accepts or stores document uploads. Documents from earlier versions have been deleted from our live systems. Any remaining backup copies are removed within 90 days.
  • AI consent records and health acknowledgements: kept until you delete your account, then removed with the rest of your data.
  • Verification and reset codes: short-lived and discarded after use or expiry.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: up to 7 years where needed for tax, accounting and legal obligations. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records under their own policies.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

11. Deleting your account

In the app: open your account settings and choose Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email Pack.Nap.App@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pack & Nap account" from the email address on your account.

You can also delete individual items (a family member, a trip, a photo, a recap, your emergency card details) without deleting your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, and photos open only for signed-in members of the trip, through app links, or through a recap link you choose to share.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

13. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers store and process data in the United States and may process it in other countries. If you use Pack & Nap from the UK, EU, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or elsewhere, your information is transferred to the US. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or a provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and UK Extension). You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

14. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information. You can delete health details or turn off AI suggestions at any time without affecting the rest of the app. Email Pack.Nap.App@gmail.com. We will confirm your identity using your account email and reply within 30 days (or sooner if local law requires). We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

UK and European Union

You also have the right to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests) and to data portability. You can complain to your local data protection authority, for example the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Canada

Under PIPEDA and provincial laws, you can access and correct your information and withdraw consent. You can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

Australia

You can access and correct your information under the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

New Zealand

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can access and correct your information. You can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).
